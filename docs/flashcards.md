# Flashcards

One question a day, answered out loud the next morning.

- **Day 1:** Q: what does a hash map trade for speed? A: memory
- **Day 2:** Q: which pointer moves first in 125? A: the one on a non letter
- **Day 3:** Q: why move the shorter line in 11? A: the taller one can still win
- **Day 4:** Q: what shrinks a variable window? A: a constraint violation
- **Day 5:** Q: what does prefix[k] minus prefix[j] give? A: the sum of the range
- **Day 6:** Q: which mid avoids overflow? A: l plus (r minus l) divided by two
